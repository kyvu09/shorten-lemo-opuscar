# Treatment: One Little Seed (*Một Hạt Giống Nhỏ*)

- **Style**: Crayon Picture Book (`styles/crayon-book/STYLE.md`)
- **Format**: 1920×1080, 24 fps (drawn lines, acting, and boil stepping at 12 fps; camera, wash leading edge, and sparkles smooth at 24 fps)
- **Length**: 56.0 seconds (target 45–60 s)
- **Audience & Goal**: Young children and English beginners learning 6 core nature/weather vocabulary words (**SEED, SUN, CLOUD, RAIN, ROOTS, FLOWER**) effortlessly through a visual, emotional story.
- **Language**:
  - **Voice**: 100% slow, warm, gentle English storytelling (Kokoro offline neural voice, clear pauses so beginners absorb every word).
  - **On-screen text & subtitles**: Bilingual stack — primary **English** on top, concise **Vietnamese** translation underneath (handwritten OFL font supporting Vietnamese diacritics, Patrick Hand, ragged knock-out patch on bare paper). Each key vocabulary word is also hand-lettered directly beside its illustrated object on the page (`SEED · hạt giống`, `SUN · mặt trời`, etc.).
- **Output files**: `one-little-seed.mp4`, `poster.jpg`, `subtitles_en_vi.srt`, `TREATMENT.md`, `CREDITS`, `build.sh`.

## 1. Three Candidate Structures (and Choice)

### Candidate A — *The Cross-Section Garden Page* ("One Little Seed") — **CHOSEN**
One generous vertical-feel picture-book page split by a hand-drawn earth horizon into sky above and soil below. Opens in extreme close-up as a brown crayon draws a tiny sleeping **seed** underground, then pulls out to reveal Leo trying to help it grow. Leo colours a blazing **sun** — comical failure (*"Too hot!"* + full silence). Leo draws a puffy blue **cloud**; a lush **sap-green watercolour rain wash** sweeps from sky through the soil, activating **wax resist** to reveal invisible white-wax **rain** above and hidden branching **roots** underground. A wordless upward crane tracks the green stem shooting up into a tall red **flower**, ending with a pull-out past the paper edge to show the finished page taped onto a sunny kitchen fridge.
- *Why chosen*: Turns the medium's core physical magic (**colouring as action** + **wax resist** uncovering underground roots) directly into the story's turning point and the vocabulary lesson's aha-moment. Every one of the 6 English words has a vivid causal link, clear child-logic humour, and a rich camera journey.

### Candidate B — *Flip-Book Picnic Basket* ("Red Apple, Yellow Banana")
Four separate pages linked by page turns introducing food words (*Apple, Banana, Bread, Bird*). Clean list pacing, but episodic with a weaker emotional turn and no organic narrative need for wax resist.

### Candidate C — *Stormy Hat Walk* ("Leo's Red Hat")
Horizontal left-to-right journey across a wide page teaching weather and clothing adjectives (*Red Hat, Big Sun, Cold Rain, Warm*). Sweet story, but depends on repetitive walk cycles and gives up the striking underground-roots wax-resist reveal.

## 2. Logline & Story Arc

- **Logline**: When little Leo tries to wake a tiny sleeping seed by colouring the sun too bright, he discovers that a crayon cloud and a cool wash of rain unlock hidden underground roots — and a tall red flower.
- **Setup (`0–17.5 s`)**: A brown crayon draws a tiny sleeping **seed** underground. Leo wants a flower, so he excitedly colours a huge smiling **sun**.
- **Turn (`17.5–41.5 s`)**: The sun is *too hot* — Leo sweats and the seed hides deeper under the dirt (comical beat of silence). Leo switches to a blue crayon, draws a big **cloud**, and pauses in hushed anticipation. A cool sap-green wash of **rain** paints down the page, magically uncovering hidden white-wax raindrops and branching underground **roots** drinking the water!
- **Ending (`41.5–56.0 s`)**: In a joyful wordless musical passage, a tall red **flower** (echoing Leo's red jumper) shoots up and blooms beside Leo. The camera pulls out past the torn edge of the page to reveal the finished illustrated vocabulary drawing taped onto a sunlit kitchen fridge — echoing the tiny seed now standing tall.

## 3. Benchmark (Grammar Only)

- **Reference works**: Eric Carle's *The Tiny Seed* (picture book) and Oliver Jeffers' *Lost and Found* (2008 short film).
- **What to learn**:
  - Rhythmic, unhurried pairing of one simple spoken phrase with one unmistakable silhouette on lots of open breathing space.
  - Patient comedic timing when a child's first idea backfires (holding on the reaction rather than rushing past it).
  - Tender, small-scale wonder where tiny natural moments feel monumental.
- **What not to take**: Collage textures, Carle's exact text/layouts, Jeffers' character designs, melodies, or any famous-book wording.

## 4. Crayon Box & Material Rules

- **Paper**: Warm off-white (`#f4efe3`), anchored paper tooth with three-octave value noise + horizontal fibre, gently embossed from top-left, warm vignette.
- **10-crayon box** (all ≤ 12, outline in warm cocoa brown — never black):
  1. **Cocoa Brown** `#472e1e` — outlines, soil horizon, Leo's tufted hair, text
  2. **Earth Ochre** `#c88a3b` — seed shell, soil pebbles, wooden garden pot/fence
  3. **Sunshine Yellow** `#f2be2c` — sun, rain boots, flower center, star sparkles
  4. **Apple Red** `#d94436` — Leo's knitted jumper & final blooming flower petals (story echo accent)
  5. **Coral Pink** `#e8877d` — rosy cheeks, tiny buds
  6. **Meadow Green** `#5a9c46` — grass tufts, growing stem, unfurling leaves
  7. **Sky Blue** `#5da7d6` — cloud highlight, Leo's dungarees, watering can
  8. **Rain Periwinkle** `#6b7ca8` — puffy raincloud belly, cool accents
  9. **Warm Peach** `#f5cca7` — Leo's face & mitten hands
  10. **White Wax** `#ffffff` — invisible wax-resist raindrops, underground branching **roots**, eye catchlights, dew sparkles
- **One watercolour wash**: **Sap Rain-Green** (`#3b785c`) sweeping smoothly across the sky and underground earth during Shot 6, repelled by the white wax so raindrops and roots shine crisp paper-white. Leo is composited *after* the wash so he never turns muddy.

## 6. Second-by-Second Beat Sheet

- `0.0–1.8`: Underground close-up; cocoa crayon draws soil hollow & ochre **seed**; tiny sleeping `z z` curls appear.
- `1.8–2.5`: Title *One Little Seed* / *Một Hạt Giống Nhỏ* writes letter-by-letter above the soil line.
- `2.6–4.6`: Voice 1: *"Here is a little seed."* (`SEED · hạt giống` label draws at `3.8 s`).
- `5.2–7.2`: Voice 2: *"It is fast asleep."* Camera holds seed gently boiling; J-cut ukulele & boot footsteps enter at `7.4 s`.
- `8.0–9.2`: Camera tilts up past the title into the sunny garden; Leo draws in / poses beside soil mound.
- `9.0–11.4`: Voice 3: *"Leo wants a flower."* Leo taps his chin, then sparkles with an idea (`11.2 s`).
- `12.2–17.0`: Camera tilts up-right; big smiling **sun** draws and zig-zag colours bright yellow (`SUN · mặt trời` at `13.4 s`). Voice 4 (`12.6–15.6 s`): *"Wake up, seed! Here comes the sun."*
- `17.5–19.6`: Locked MCU; orange heat waves wobble; Leo fans his face (sweat drop pops); underground seed squints and pulls dirt blanket tighter. Bassoon slides down.
- `19.7–21.1`: **SILENCE #1** (boil calms to half). Tiny crayon set-down *clink* at `20.2 s`.
- `20.4–22.4`: Voice 5: *"Oh no! Too hot!"* At `22.3 s` Leo grabs blue crayon with wide bright eyes.
- `23.5–28.3`: Camera trucks across sky; puffy **cloud** draws live and shades in blue/periwinkle, sliding partially across the sun (`CLOUD · đám mây` at `25.0 s`). Voice 6 (`24.0–26.8 s`): *"Leo draws a big blue cloud."*
- `28.4–29.6`: **SILENCE #2** — cloud puffs cheeks, music drops out, boil calms to half. At `29.6 s` J-cut glockenspiel drop + wet brush touch!
- `30.0–36.5`: Wide cross-section held locked; sap-green watercolour wash sweeps downward across sky & soil. Invisible white-wax raindrops (`RAIN · cơn mưa` at `31.0 s`) and underground branching roots (`ROOTS · rễ cây` at `35.2 s`) appear like magic!
- `30.6–32.8`: Voice 7: *"Down comes the cool rain!"*
- `35.2–38.0`: Voice 8: *"Look! Hidden roots drink the water!"*
- `38.5–41.5`: Seed opens big sparkling eyes, grins, cracks a vivid green sprout through the earth!
- `41.5–47.5`: **Wordless musical bloom** (no subtitles). Camera follows the green stem shooting upward, two leaves unfurling, and a huge red smiling **flower** scribbling open; Leo jumps twice on the beat!
- `47.5–50.8`: `FLOWER · bông hoa` draws beside the flower. Voice 9 (`47.8–50.2 s`): *"Pop! A tall red flower!"*
- `50.8–56.0`: Smooth pull-out past the paper edge onto the sunlit mint fridge with masking tape & magnets. Voice 10 (`51.6–54.0 s`): *"Well done, little seed."* Gentle chime & birdsong L-cut ring out to `56.0 s`.

## 7. Cue Map (Music Grid First)

- **Key**: F Major (warm, bright, nursery-book open timbre; shifts briefly to D minor tint during "Too hot!" sigh, resolving joyfully back to F Major for the bloom).
- **Base Tempo**: **108 BPM** (4/4; beat = 0.5556 s; bar = 2.2222 s), with a natural breath/rubato feel in the wash section (96 BPM feel) and complete silences at Bars 9–10 and Bar 13.
- **Instrumentation (all CC0 small wooden & metal-toothed instruments — zero strings, zero grand piano)**:
  - `ukulele` (FreePats CC0) — sunny fingerpicked arpeggios & gentle strums
  - `recorder` (VCSL CC0) — singing wooden lead melody (held back in full until the wordless flower bloom!)
  - `bassoon` / `bassoon_stac` (VSCO 2 CE CC0) — playful walking bass & comical downward sigh
  - `glockenspiel` (VCSL CC0) — sparkling single drops for wax-resist rain & uncovered roots
  - `marimba` (VCSL CC0) — soft wooden pulse & bouncy syllable/pop accents
  - `hand_chimes` (VCSL CC0) — warm radiant bloom chord when the red flower opens
  - `woodblock` + `shaker` (VCSL CC0) — crisp handmade storybook rhythm

## 9. Subtitle & Typography Design

- **Font**: **Patrick Hand** (Google Fonts, OFL — full Latin + Vietnamese diacritics) for both printed book subtitles and clean vocabulary labels; **Mali Bold** (Google Fonts, OFL — warm child-handwriting bold display face with full Vietnamese diacritics!) for the main hand-lettered title (*One Little Seed / Một Hạt Giống Nhỏ*) and bold uppercase English vocabulary tags (`SEED`, `SUN`, `CLOUD`, `RAIN`, `ROOTS`, `FLOWER`).
- **Printed book subtitles (bottom-centre)**:
  - Positioned on a ragged knock-out patch of bare warm paper (`#f4efe3`) with generous open margin below the action (never a dark box, never covering Leo or the seed/roots).
  - Top line: **English** (`50 px` Patrick Hand, cocoa brown `#472e1e`, with the key vocabulary word accented in its matching crayon colour when spoken!).
  - Bottom line: **Vietnamese** (`30 px` Patrick Hand, warm cocoa `#6e4e39`).
  - Animates left→right in `0.35 s`, boils gently on twos (12 fps), disappears cleanly during the wordless bloom (`41.5–47.5 s`).
  - Every subtitle holds at least `max(2.7 s, speech + 0.8 s)`. Exported alongside as `subtitles_en_vi.srt`.
- **On-page vocabulary tags**: Drawn directly beside each object in the scene (`SEED` + `hạt giống`, etc.), reported in `window.TEXTS(t)` so `readcheck.mjs` verifies comfortable reading time for every label.
